❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about gameplay, saving & classroom use
"Is this game like Prodigy?" ▾

Yes, in spirit! Like Prodigy, MathLand blends a fantasy world with math challenges that scale by skill level—covering addition & subtraction, multiplication & division, and linear equations—while awarding XP and coins so scholars can level up and gear up.

"This is similar to Animal Crossing" ▾

Spot on! Shaking trees for surprise coins or angry bee swarms, running from bees or snagging them with a bug net, chatting with quirky NPCs like Tinker Pip, and getting lectured by Mr. Reseth when you exit without saving draw playful inspiration from cozy life-sim classics.

Why do students need to click "Save Progress"? ▾

Clicking Save Progress in the Scholar Portal saves a student's Level, XP, coins, tools, and position to Firestore. Closing or refreshing without saving marks the session unsaved and sends Mr. Reseth out to remind them!

How do I print student login cards for class? ▾

Add your scholars using their First Name, Last Initial, and Grade, set your session focus and XP goal, and click Print Student Login to open a print-ready sheet of scholar pass cards.
