Safe & Habit-Building

For Parents

Give your child an engaging, low-pressure way to practice math at home while building helpful habits around saving progress and taking responsibility.

  • ▸ Positive Math Reinforcement: Solving runes earns XP, levels, and coins—turning extra practice into exciting milestones.
  • ▸ Teaches Saving & Accountability: Forgetting to save before exiting triggers humorous visits from Mr. Reseth, helping kids remember to save their work.
  • ▸ Easy Sign-In Options: Scholars can sign in with email or Google so their saved level, XP, coins, and tools stay ready for the next session.
